Workout tracker for Android and Wear OS

Your training. Your progress. Your goal.

GymWerk plans your sessions, logs every set with weight and reps, and shows you how you get stronger. On your phone and right on your wrist. No account, no ads.

Get it on Google PlayFree, no ads

  • Training plans

    Your own plans with tags and colors. Exercises from the library or created yourself, sorted by muscle group.

  • Track every set

    Log weight and reps in seconds, with a burn meter and weight suggestions from your last workout.

  • Wear OS

    Check off sets from your wrist, see the rest countdown and skip rests. Your phone stays in your pocket.

  • See your progress

    Complete history, weight charts and personal records for every exercise.

  • Backup

    Export all data as a file and restore it on a new device.

  • No account

    No email, no password, no tracking. Your workout data never leaves your device.
